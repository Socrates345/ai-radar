# Sources

## Primary Sources

Scouting is driven by personal research supplemented by the following channels:

- [The AI Search](https://www.youtube.com/@theAIsearch) — news and tool discovery channel focused on making the latest AI developments accessible; strong coverage of newly released models and demos.
- [MrEflow](https://www.youtube.com/@mreflow) — practical AI news and workflow breakdowns; covers recent model releases with a hands-on perspective.

## Landscape

Related projects in the same space — and why this one is different:

| Project | What it is | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [mahseema/awesome-ai-tools](https://github.com/mahseema/awesome-ai-tools) | Community-maintained GitHub awesome-list, broad coverage across all AI categories | Hundreds of shallow entries; no chronological tracking, no local/research distinction |
| [futuretools.io](https://futuretools.io) | Curated searchable database + newsletter by Matt Wolfe | Discovery-focused, generalist, no depth on research-grade or offline tools |
| [theresanaiforthat.com](https://theresanaiforthat.com) | Massive directory (49k+ tools), task and model search | Pure directory; breadth over everything; no curation philosophy |

**What this list does differently:** depth over breadth. Every entry is personally evaluated. The focus is on niche domains that aggregators skip (3D, audio, OCR, cybersecurity), with explicit tagging for local/open-source tools, dated entries for chronological tracking, and inclusion of academic research tools alongside commercial products.
