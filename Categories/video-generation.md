# Video Generation

> Personal selection of the best video generation tools at their respective release dates. Sorted newest first within each section.

### Text-to-Video
#### [LTX-2.5](https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.5) — `08.2026` — `open-source` `local` `free`
Lightricks' 22B-parameter open-weight audio-video diffusion transformer; fastest open-source video generator at release, with native multishot scenes and 4K HDR output. *(supersedes LTX-2.3)*
#### [MiniMax H3](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3) — `08.2026` — `open-source` `local` `free`
MiniMax's general-purpose multimodal video model (33B params, open weights) — ranks top-3 across text-to-video, image-to-video, and video editing on Artificial Analysis; ships with a distilled FastH3 variant for faster inference.
#### [Seedance 2.5](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog/one-take-creation-flexible-referencing-introducing-seedance-2-5) — `07.2026` — `online` `paid`
ByteDance's top-tier commercial video generation model; adds one-take 30-second generation and multimodal referencing across up to 30 images, 10 videos, and 10 audio clips. *(supersedes Seedance 2.0)*
#### [FLUX 3 Video](https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3-video) — `07.2026` — `online`
Black Forest Labs' single "Self-Flow" backbone generating up to 20-second video with natively synced audio; currently early-access/waitlist only.
#### [MobileWan](https://qualcomm-ai-research.github.io/MobileWan/) — `07.2026` — `open-source` `local` `research`
Qualcomm's on-device video diffusion system — runs a 5B-parameter model directly on consumer mobile hardware.
#### [Warp-as-History](https://yyfz.github.io/warp-as-history/) — `05.2026` — `open-source` `research`
Camera-controlled video generation — converts camera movements into visual history for pretrained video models to interpret, requiring only a single annotated training example.
#### [Gemini Omni](https://deepmind.google/) — `05.2026` — `online`
Google DeepMind's any-to-any generative model — fuses Gemini reasoning with Veo rendering, Genie world simulation, and Nano Banana image editing into one multi-turn-editable video pipeline.
#### [CausalCine](https://yihao-meng.github.io/CausalCine/) — `05.2026` — `research`
Generates multi-shot video narratives in real time via autoregressive methods — interactive shot-level prompting with consistent narrative continuity.
#### [Kling 3.0](https://app.klingai.com/) — `02.2026` — `online` `paid`
Major version release of Kling's commercial video generation platform.
#### [VerSeCrafter](https://github.com/TencentARC/VerseCrafter) — `02.2026` — `open-source`
Tencent's text-to-video model with controllable camera movement.
#### [FlowAct R1](https://grisoon.github.io/FlowAct-R1/) — `01.2026` — `research`
Real-time framework for interactive, controllable humanoid video generation at 25fps with infinite-length streaming, built on an MMDiT diffusion backbone.
#### [LTX-2](https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-2) — `01.2026` — `open-source` `local` `free`
Best local open-source video generator at release; produces 4K long-form video with audio at low resource cost.
#### [Wan 2.6](https://wan.video) — `12.2025` — `open-source` `local` `free`
Alibaba's latest open-source Wan model; best uncensored local video generation at release.
#### [HunyuanVideo 1.5](https://github.com/Tencent/HunyuanVideo) — `11.2025` — `open-source` `local` `free`
Tencent's high-quality local open-source video generation model.
#### [HappyHorse](https://happyhorse.app/) — `11.2025` — `online`
Alibaba's text-to-video model (audio not supported).
#### [V-mem](https://v-mem.github.io/) — `10.2025` — `open-source` `research`
Maintains consistent characters across multiple generated video scenes.
#### [Holo-Cine](https://holo-cine.github.io/) — `10.2025` — `open-source` `research`

#### [UltraGen](https://sjtuplayer.github.io/projects/UltraGen/) — `07.2025` — `open-source` `research`

#### [Hailuo 2](https://hailuoai.video/) — `06.2025` — `online` `freemium`
