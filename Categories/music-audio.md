# Music & Audio Generation

> Personal selection of the best music generation, audio synthesis, and video-to-audio tools. Sorted newest first within each section.

### Music Generation
#### [MiniMax Music 3.0](https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-music-3-0-next-generation-open-weights-production-ready-versatile-music-model) — `08.2026` — `open-source` `local` `online` `api` `free`
MiniMax's open-weight music model — composes, arranges, and produces complete songs up to 5 minutes from a concept + optional lyrics, pairing an 8B LLM with a flow-matching synthesis stack. *(supersedes Minimax Music 2.5)*
#### [HappyShrimp](https://www.happyshrimp.ai/) — `08.2026` — `online` `free`
Alibaba's AI song generator (beta) — text-prompt-to-full-song generation (melody, arrangement, lyrics, vocals), or instrumental/custom-lyrics mode; a sibling product to the already-catalogued HappyHorse, not the same tool.
#### [MiDashengLM-Gen](https://xingws.github.io/midashenglm-gen-demo/) — `08.2026` — `research`
Generates unified audio scenes — speech, music, sound effects, and environmental audio simultaneously from text, with emotion control across nine languages.
#### [Mirelo Audio-to-MIDI](https://mirelo.ai/blog/turning-audio-to-midi) — `07.2026` — `open-source` `free` `online`
Transcribes a full music mix into separate, instrument-labeled MIDI tracks, plus detects chords/key/tempo, with one-click DAW export.
#### [Magenta Realtime 2](https://magenta.withgoogle.com/magenta-realtime-2) — `06.2026` — `open-source` `local` `free`
Google's open-source real-time music generation model.
#### [Khala](https://khala-music-ai.github.io/Khala-demo/) — `05.2026` — `open-source` `free`
High-fidelity open-source full-song generation from text descriptions and lyrics — developed at the Central Conservatory of Music; top open-source music model at release.
#### [Stable Audio 3](https://stability.ai/news-updates/meet-stable-audio-3-the-model-family-built-for-artistic-experimentation-with-open-weight-models) — `05.2026` — `open-source` `local` `free`
Stability AI's open-weight audio family for SFX and music generation — built for artistic experimentation with full local use.
#### [ACE-Step 1.5](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5) — `04.2026` — `open-source` `local` `free`
Best open-source music generation model at release.
#### [Lyria 3](https://deepmind.google/models/lyria/) — `02.2026` — `online`
Google's music generation model.
#### [HeartMula](https://github.com/HeartMuLa/heartlib) — `02.2026` — `open-source` `free`
Open-source music foundation model.
#### [SongBloom](https://cypress-yang.github.io/SongBloom_demo/) — `06.2025` — `open-source` `research`
Coherent full-song generation with consistent structure and musical logic.
#### [ACE-Step](https://ace-step.github.io/) — `06.2025` — `open-source` `local` `free`
Offline open-source music generation; predecessor to ACE-Step 1.5.
#### [Foundation-1](https://github.com/RoyalCities/RC-stable-audio-tools) — `05.2025` — `open-source` `local` `free`
Free open-source model for music production; fast, low VRAM.
#### [Adobe Firefly Audio](https://www.adobe.com/products/firefly/features/sound-effect-generator.html) — `05.2025` — `online` `paid` `platform`
Adobe Firefly's sound effects generation — strong for cinematic SFX.
#### [Stable Audio 2.5](https://stability.ai/news/stability-ai-and-arm-release-stable-audio-open-small-enabling-real-world-deployment-for-on-device-audio-control) — `04.2025` — `online` `paid`
Stability AI's enterprise audio model for professional sound production; also has a small on-device variant.
#### [Mureka O1](https://mureka.ai) — `03.2025` — `online`
Chain-of-thought AI music model — applies reasoning to music composition.
#### [InspireMusic](https://funaudiollm.github.io/inspiremusic/) — `02.2025` — `open-source` `research`
Open music/song/audio generation toolkit from Alibaba's FunAudioLLM lab, combining audio tokenizers with an autoregressive transformer and flow-matching model for long-form (up to ~8 min) text-to-music.
#### [ElevenLabs SFX](https://elevenlabs.io/) — `01.2025` — `online` `freemium`
ElevenLabs' sound effects generation models (v2).
#### [YuE](https://github.com/multimodal-art-projection/YuE) — `01.2025` — `open-source` `local` `free`
Open-source AI music generation model.
#### [Suno v6](https://suno.com/) — `09.2026` — `online` `freemium`
Suno's first licensed model family (partnered with Warner Music, BMG, Believe) — ships as v6 (flagship, Pro), v6-wild (more varied/experimental, Pro), and v6-mini (faster, free tier). *(supersedes Suno v4)*
#### [Udio](https://www.udio.com/) — `04.2024` — `online` `freemium`
AI music generation platform; pivoted to a fully licensed model in late 2025 after label settlements (UMG, WMG).
#### [Riffusion](https://www.riffusion.com/) — `12.2022` — `online` `free`
Music generation via spectrogram diffusion; strong for experimental and ambient styles.

### AI Singing
#### [VocalRender](https://pymaster17.github.io/VocalRender/) — `07.2026` — `research`
Score-native singing voice synthesis — converts composer inputs (lyrics, MIDI pitch, note values, tempo) directly into expressive singing audio without phoneme-level alignment.
#### [SoulX Singer](https://soul-ailab.github.io/soulx-singer/) — `02.2026` — `open-source` `research`
Open-source AI singing voice generation model.
#### [DiffRhythm](https://github.com/ASLP-lab/DiffRhythm) — `03.2025` — `open-source` `research`
Diffusion-based rhythm and singing generation.
