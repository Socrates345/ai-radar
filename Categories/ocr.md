# OCR & Document Understanding

> AI models for optical character recognition and structured document extraction. Early-stage category — more entries coming.

---

#### [Mistral OCR 4](https://mistral.ai/news/ocr-4/) — `06.2026` — `api` `online` `freemium` `platform`
Mistral's document-intelligence OCR model/API — converts PDFs, DOCs, and images into structured output with bounding boxes, block classification, and confidence scores across 170 languages.
#### [SenseNova U1](https://github.com/OpenSenseNova/SenseNova-U1) — `05.2026` — `open-source` `research`
Comprehensive multimodal model with strong OCR and document understanding capabilities.

#### [MiniCPM-V 4.6](https://huggingface.co/openbmb/MiniCPM-V-4.6/tree/main) — `05.2026` — `open-source` `local` `free`
Lightweight on-device multimodal model (2.6 GB) with strong OCR and image understanding; designed to run on mobile hardware without a GPU.

#### [GLM-OCR](https://github.com/zai-org/GLM-OCR) — `02.2026` — `open-source`
Zhipu AI's dedicated OCR model.

#### [LightOn OCR](https://huggingface.co/lightonai/LightOnOCR-1B-1025) — `02.2026` — `open-source` `local` `free`
Compact 1B OCR model with strong performance on structured documents.

#### [Step3-VL](https://github.com/stepfun-ai/Step3-VL-10B) — `02.2026` — `open-source` `research`
Strong vision-language model with document and OCR understanding.

#### [GOT-OCR2](https://huggingface.co/stepfun-ai/GOT-OCR2_0) — `09.2024` — `open-source` `local` `free`
Advanced OCR model handling unstructured documents and complex layouts.
